# TecAlliance Brand Guidelines 2026

Comprehensive reference for TecAlliance brand identity, visual design, communication, and compliance.

---

## Brand Identity

### Brand Essence

**Tagline:** Connecting the Aftermarket

TecAlliance enables the Independent Automotive Aftermarket (IAM) to function as a connected, interoperable system through industry standards and neutral data. As a trusted infrastructure provider, TecAlliance defines and maintains the foundations that allow independent actors to coordinate, collaborate, and operate at scale.

### Purpose

TecAlliance addresses industry-wide challenges in the independent automotive aftermarket that no single player can solve alone. The company enables value creation through structured, shared, and validated data and enabling services.

### Vision

TecAlliance empowers the IAM to shape a connected data ecosystem, safeguarding independence through trusted standards and a shared data foundation that drives sustainable global business growth.

### Mission

TecAlliance unlocks growth and ensures the competitiveness of the IAM by establishing neutral standards and enabling market connectivity, built on trusted data elements and core enabling functions.

### Positioning

TecAlliance helps IAM players operate more effectively and grow more sustainably by offering access to standardised, validated, and interoperable data. This drives real-world productivity gains and collaboration without increasing complexity.

### Differentiators

- **Neutrality:** Trusted by all because TecAlliance is not in competition with market participants.
- **Market Ownership:** Backed by key manufacturers and market players and jointly owned by those it serves.
- **Standards Authority:** TecAlliance does not only follow standards; it defines and implements them across the industry.
- **Focus on Relevance:** Delivers relevance to the market as a whole, solving real problems rather than isolated needs.
- **Platform Independence:** Data can be used in any system. TecAlliance does not lock anyone into its technology.
- **Decades of Operational Proof:** TecAlliance has already solved major systemic industry problems.
- **Enablement, Not Delivery:** TecAlliance enables others to build solutions on top of its standards, data, and infrastructure.
- **Market Connector:** TecAlliance serves as connectivity infrastructure for the IAM.

### Brand Personality

**TecAlliance Is:** Collaborative and approachable, reliable and consistent, experienced and practical, calm and confident, a connector, strategically customer-aware, enabling and humble, precise and data-driven, clear and structured, human, understanding the market from within, the IAM's voice infrastructure, a force for enablement, stable but evolving.

**TecAlliance Is Not:** Product-obsessed, vague, theatrical, overpromising, arrogant, technocratic, exclusive, fragmented, defensive, isolated, inward-focused.

## Communication and Voice

### Communication Standards

- **Tone:** Clear, confident, inclusive
- **Style:** Informative, structured, purpose-driven
- **Language:** British English
- **Voice:** Use "we" for TecAlliance
- **Audience address:** Use "you" where context allows
- **Principle:** No jargon, no exaggeration, clarity and precision above all

### Writing Rules

1. Use British English spelling.
2. Write from the perspective of "we".
3. Address the reader as "you" when appropriate.
4. Be clear, structured, and purposeful.
5. Communicate with conviction, not arrogance.
6. Avoid buzzwords, inflated language, and vague claims.
7. Make every message useful and action-oriented.
8. Avoid empty phrases.
9. Be customer-aware without being overly promotional.

### Colour Hierarchy

- **Pure White** `#FFFFFF`: ~60% (dominant base)
- **Burnout Rubber** `#2E2E2E` / **Circuit Slate** `#4B5568`: ~30% (structure and readability)
- **Ignition Mango** `#FF8700`: ~10% (accent, highlights, calls to action)

### Colour Usage Rules

1. Use Pure White as the main background colour whenever possible.
2. Use Burnout Rubber for primary typography.
3. Use Circuit Slate for structured areas, supporting panels, dark interface elements.
4. Use Ignition Mango sparingly for highlights, calls to action, accents.
5. Use product colours only when representing the relevant product identity.
6. Do not substitute approximate colours.
7. Do not invent additional brand colours unless explicitly approved.
8. Ensure all foreground/background combinations meet accessibility contrast requirements.

## Layout System

### Layout Principles

Clean, professional, accessible, balanced, structured, easy to read.

### Layout Construction

The TecAlliance logo is used as the foundational guide for layout calculations.

- **Portrait formats:** `Y = document width / 5`
- **Landscape formats:** `Y = document width / 7`
- After Y is known, X is derived from the logo construction for consistent margins.

#### Portrait Margins: `1.5X` on all sides.
#### Landscape Margins: Left/Right `1.5X`, Top/Bottom `1X`.

### Text Layout Rules

- Use left alignment for natural reading flow.
- Use generous line spacing and paragraph spacing.
- Maintain clear hierarchy between headlines and body text.
- Avoid all-caps headlines.
- Keep layouts approachable and clear.

## Visual Identity

### Logo System

#### Primary Logo

- **Print:** 20 mm wide minimum
- **Digital:** 120 px wide minimum
- Maintain proper clear space and legibility.
- Do not distort, recolour, rotate, stretch, compress, rearrange, or alter the logo.

#### Product Logos (TecDoc, TecCom, TecRMI)

- Small-scale rule: use logo without text when ≤65 px (digital) or ≤15 mm (print).
- Monochrome version: exception only, requires minimum contrast `7.5:1`, solid background, Marketing approval.
- Always display TecAlliance logo alongside product logos, separated with a vertical line.

#### Solution Lockups

- Product name in **Barlow Condensed Semibold** (first line).
- Solution or programme below in **Inter Light**.
- New or exceptional cases require Marketing approval.

### Imagery Style

- Clean technical line drawings with photorealistic elements.
- Primary corporate colours (Ignition Mango, Circuit Slate, Burnout Rubber, Pure White).
- Avoid generic stock images.
- People: photorealistic on technical line art backgrounds.
- Mockups: use only when contextually valuable; prefer clean screenshot sections with rounded corners.

### Icons

- Styles: Lined, Solid, Duotone, TA Duotone (preferred).
- Maintain uniform sizing and consistent spacing.
- Use brand colours: Ignition Mango, Circuit Slate, Burnout Rubber, Pure White.

## Brand Applications

### Co-Branding Types

- **Endorsement:** TecAlliance acknowledges/supports a collaborator (unequal weight).
- **Co-Communication:** Equal collaboration, balanced visibility.
- **Co-Creation:** Jointly developed product or service.

### Certification Badges

- **TecDoc:** Data Supplier, Certified Data Supplier, Premier Data Supplier.
- **TecCom:** Certified, Premium (4.5–5 stars), Member.
- **TAPP:** Certified resellers, integrators, consultants, software vendors.
- **Inside Badges:** For certified customers of TecDoc, TecCom, TecRMI data solutions.

### Badge Sizing

- **Print:** Minimum height 15 mm
- **Digital:** Minimum height 150 px
- Clear space: use orange square (Ignition Mango `#FF8700`) as reference.

### Badge Backgrounds

- Preferred: Pure White `#FFFFFF`.
- Monochrome: exception only, minimum contrast `7.5:1`, solid dark background, Marketing approval required.

### Badge Validity

Badges may only be used while certification status is active. Remove from all materials if expired or revoked.

## Accessibility

### Standards

- European Accessibility Act
- EN 301 549
- WCAG 2.2 Level AA

### Contrast Requirements

- **Small text:** minimum `4.5:1`
- **Large text:** minimum `3:1`
- **UI components:** minimum `3:1`

### Practical Guidance

- Burnout Rubber on Pure White = high contrast (safe for body text).
- Circuit Slate on Pure White = verify ratio (may be borderline for small text).
- Ignition Mango on Pure White = low contrast (do NOT use for body text; accent only).
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.
- Always test foreground/background pairs against WCAG 2.2 AA thresholds.

## AI Agent Instructions

### General Principle

Whether content is created manually or with AI support, every visual, message, and asset must reflect TecAlliance brand standards. AI-generated content is not exempt from brand compliance.

### Brand Meaning

- Present TecAlliance as a neutral infrastructure provider for the IAM.
- Emphasise connectivity, interoperability, standards, neutral data, and trusted enablement.
- Do not position TecAlliance as competing with market participants.
- Avoid presenting TecAlliance as a single end-solution provider.

### Visual Defaults

When no specific instruction is given:

- Background: Pure White `#FFFFFF`
- Primary text: Burnout Rubber `#2E2E2E`
- Structural elements: Circuit Slate `#4B5568`
- Accent: Ignition Mango `#FF8700`

### Design Constraints

- Always check accessibility contrast.
- Keep content left-aligned unless layout requires otherwise.
- Avoid all-caps headlines.
- Use generous spacing.
- Use Barlow / Barlow Condensed for headlines, Inter for body text.
- Use Ignition Mango sparingly as an accent.

### Approval Constraints

Marketing approval is required for: monochrome product logos, monochrome solution lockups, monochrome badges, new or exceptional product lockup cases, any non-standard brand application.
