# TecAlliance Accessibility Requirements

Source: TecAlliance Brand Guidelines 2026

## Standards

TecAlliance aligns materials with:

- European Accessibility Act requirements
- EN 301 549
- WCAG 2.2 Level AA

## Contrast Requirements

- **Small text:** minimum contrast ratio `4.5:1`
- **Large text:** minimum contrast ratio `3:1`
- **UI components:** minimum contrast ratio `3:1`

## Key Contrast Values from Brand Book

The following contrast examples are documented:

- `5.63:1`
- `1.8:1`
- `2.92:1`
- `2.62:1`
- `2.67:1`
- `2.4:1`

- `4.64:1`
- `5.17:1`
- `5.08:1`

**Important:** Not all brand colour combinations are accessible. Always validate contrast before finalising any design.

## Accessibility Rules

1. Check colour contrast for all text.
2. Use at least `4.5:1` for small text.
3. Use at least `3:1` for large text and UI components.
4. Prefer high-contrast combinations involving:
   - Burnout Rubber `#2E2E2E`
   - Pure White `#FFFFFF`
   - Circuit Slate `#4B5568`
5. Use Ignition Mango `#FF8700` mainly as an accent, not as long-form text.
6. Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.

## Practical Guidance

When implementing UI or generating content:

- Always test foreground/background pairs against WCAG 2.2 AA thresholds.
- Burnout Rubber on Pure White = high contrast (safe for body text).
- Circuit Slate on Pure White = verify ratio (may be borderline for small text).
- Ignition Mango on Pure White = low contrast (do NOT use for body text; accent only).
- Ignition Mango on Burnout Rubber = verify ratio before use.
