# TecAlliance Visual Identity

Source: TecAlliance Brand Guidelines 2026

## Logo System

### Primary Logo

The primary logo consists of the TecAlliance logomark alongside the company name. It is the main identifier of the brand.

#### Minimum Sizes

- **Print:** 20 mm wide minimum
- **Digital:** 120 px wide minimum

#### Usage Rules

- Maintain proper clear space.
- Maintain legibility across all applications.
- Use approved logo versions only.
- Do not distort, recolour, rotate, stretch, compress, rearrange, or alter the logo.

### Logo Mark

The logo mark is a standalone representation used when space is limited or when brand recognition has already been established.

- Uses the golden ratio: `B = A / 1.618`, `C = B / 1.618`

### Product Logos

Product logos exist for: TecDoc, TecCom, TecRMI.

#### Small-Scale Rule

For small-scale applications, use the logo without text when the logo is:

- **Digital:** `<= 65 px`
- **Print:** `<= 15 mm`

#### Monochrome Rule

A monochrome version may be used only as an exception under these conditions:

- Dark background with minimum contrast ratio of `7.5:1`.
- Solid background only.
- No contrasted gradients, patterns, or additional graphic elements.
- Prior alignment with Marketing is required.

### Logo Combination Rule

The TecAlliance logo should always appear alongside any product logo.

- Display both logos together, separated with a vertical line.
- For special formats, logos may be placed separately within the layout.

### Solution Lockups

- Product name on the first line in **Barlow Condensed Semibold**.
- Solution or programme below in **Inter Light**.
- Products outside a defined product family are positioned under TecAlliance.
- New or exceptional cases require Marketing approval.

## Imagery

### Overall Imagery Style

The preferred imagery style combines:

- Clean technical line drawings
- Primary corporate colours (Ignition Mango, Circuit Slate, Burnout Rubber, Pure White)
- Dynamic Ignition Mango accents `#FF8700`
- Photorealistic elements
- A modern, precise, professional appearance

Avoid generic stock images or unrelated photography.

### People Imagery

- Individuals should appear photorealistic.
- Backgrounds should remain in technical line art style.
- Colour tones should align with the corporate palette.
- Black-and-white representations are acceptable.

### Real Imagery

Real imagery may be used for events, meetings, quotes, and authentic documentation.

- Use the highest possible quality.
- Select clean, professional images.
- Where feasible, remove backgrounds and replace with line-art backgrounds and colour accents.
- Alternatively, frame imagery within clean rectangular or square shapes.

### Product Visual Identity

Product colours are used exclusively within product logos and stock imagery:

- TecDoc / Blueprint: `#0A74DA`
- TecCom / Rapid Red: `#D22630`
- TecRMI / Torque Teal: `#007A87`

### Mockups and Screenshots

- Use device mockups only when they provide clear contextual value.
- Prefer clean sections of screenshots without unnecessary device frames.
- Use rounded corners where appropriate.
- Highlight the most relevant part of the interface.
- Avoid crowded or unreadable screens.

## Icons

### Icon Styles

- Lined
- Solid
- Duotone
- TA Duotone (preferred whenever possible)

### Icon Rules

- Maintain uniform sizing.
- Maintain consistent spacing.
- Use icons consistently across applications.
- Prefer brand colours: Ignition Mango `#FF8700`, Circuit Slate `#4B5568`, Burnout Rubber `#2E2E2E`, Pure White `#FFFFFF`.
