# TecAlliance Communication and Voice

Source: TecAlliance Brand Guidelines 2026

## Voice

TecAlliance communication is clear, professional, and driven by expertise in data solutions. It uses approachable, informative language that reflects the brand values and maintains a consistent, customer-focused approach.

### Communication Standards

- **Tone:** Clear, confident, inclusive
- **Style:** Informative, structured, purpose-driven
- **Language:** British English
- **Voice:** Use "we" for TecAlliance
- **Audience address:** Use "you" where context allows
- **Principle:** No jargon, no exaggeration, clarity and precision above all

### Writing Rules

When writing as TecAlliance:

1. Use British English spelling.
2. Write from the perspective of "we".
3. Address the reader as "you" when appropriate.
4. Be clear, structured, and purposeful.
5. Communicate with conviction, not arrogance.
6. Avoid buzzwords, inflated language, and vague claims.
7. Make every message useful and action-oriented.
8. Avoid empty phrases.
9. Be customer-aware without being overly promotional.

## Visual Communication

Visual communication examples include:

- Layout alternatives
- Social media
- Brand collateral
- Online meeting backgrounds
- Approved branding material
- Events and brand presence

Marketing primarily creates these layouts. Frequent content creators may request access to Canva Enterprise, subject to approval from their line manager.

### Events and Brand Presence

At events, TecAlliance should be the leading brand presence. Product brands remain visible when relevant, especially at solution-focused events where the product identity may share the spotlight.
