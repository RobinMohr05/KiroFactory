# TecAlliance Brand Applications

Source: TecAlliance Brand Guidelines 2026

## Co-Branding

TecAlliance uses standardised branding in collaborations to ensure clear and consistent communication. All brand principles apply to co-branded materials.

### Co-Branding Types

#### Endorsement

TecAlliance acknowledges or supports a collaborator (unequally weighted). Examples: Powered by, In cooperation with, Supported by.

#### Co-Communication

An equal collaboration where both brands are represented with balanced visibility. Each brand keeps its own voice and identity.

#### Co-Creation

A collaboration that results in a jointly developed product or service. Guidelines are established collaboratively with the other company.

### Co-Branding Decision Logic

Use co-branding only when it has a clear purpose. Questions to consider:

1. Does TecAlliance benefit from communicating the collaboration?
2. Do both parties contribute at an equal level?
3. Is TecAlliance playing the main role?
4. Is there a common product, service, event, or similar result?
5. Is TecAlliance the publisher of the communication medium?

## Certification Badges

Certified partners may use official certification badges across digital and print materials. Badges reinforce trust and verified status within the ecosystem.

### Badge Use Cases

Websites, marketing assets, trade show materials, email signatures, videos, social media, PDF documents.

### TecDoc Data Supplier Badges

- TecDoc Data Supplier
- Certified Data Supplier
- Premier Data Supplier

Badges may only be used while the certification status remains active.

### TecCom Badges (TecCom Excellence Program)

- Certified badge: for certified suppliers
- Premium badge: for suppliers achieving 4.5 to 5 stars
- Member badge: for suppliers and buyers actively using TecCom

### Badge Sizes

- **Print:** Minimum height `15 mm`
- **Digital:** Minimum height `150 px`

### Badge Clear Space

- Use the orange square in the logo as the minimum clear-space reference.
- Orange square colour: **Ignition Mango** `#FF8700`.

### Badge Validity

Certification badges may only be used while certification status is valid. If certification expires or is revoked, the badge must be removed from all materials.

### Badge Backgrounds

- Preferred: Pure White `#FFFFFF`
- If not possible: use JPEG version with integrated white background.
- Monochrome version only with prior Marketing approval.

## "Inside" Badges

Certified customers of TecDoc, TecCom, or TecRMI data solutions are entitled to use the corresponding "Inside" badge.

### Inside Badge Requirements

- Digital minimum height: `150 px`
- Use a white or very light background (preferred: `#FFFFFF`).
- Clear space: top/bottom = half the orange square; left/right = full orange square.
- Orange square: Ignition Mango `#FF8700`.

### Copyright Requirement

Display must be used together with:

`(c) 2026 TecAlliance. All rights reserved.`

For TecDoc, the logo may link to: `https://www.tecalliance.net/copyright-note/`

## TAPP Badges

TecAlliance Partner Program badges for certified resellers, integrators, consultants, and software vendors.

### TAPP Badge Sizes

- **Print:** Minimum height `15 mm`
- **Digital:** Minimum height `150 px`

### TAPP Badge Clear Space

- Top/bottom: half of the orange square
- Left/right: full size of the orange square
- Orange square: Ignition Mango `#FF8700`

### TAPP Badge Backgrounds

- Preferred: Pure White `#FFFFFF`
- Monochrome version only with prior Marketing approval.

## Monochrome Badges

Full-colour badges may not always provide optimal visibility on dark backgrounds. Monochromatic white version as exception only.

### Conditions

- Minimum contrast ratio: `7.5:1`
- Solid background only
- No contrasted gradients or patterns
- Marketing approval required
- Colour: Pure White `#FFFFFF` on sufficiently dark solid backgrounds
