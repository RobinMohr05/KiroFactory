# TecAlliance AI Agent Instructions

Source: TecAlliance Brand Guidelines 2026

## General Principle

Whether content is created manually or with AI support, every visual, message, and asset must reflect TecAlliance brand standards, voice, visual identity, accessibility requirements, and governance principles. AI-generated content is not exempt from brand compliance.

## Brand Meaning

- Present TecAlliance as a neutral infrastructure provider for the IAM.
- Emphasise connectivity, interoperability, standards, neutral data, and trusted enablement.
- Do not position TecAlliance as competing with market participants.
- Avoid presenting TecAlliance as a single end-solution provider. It enables others to build and operate effectively.

## Voice and Writing

- Use British English.
- Write as "we" when speaking on behalf of TecAlliance.
- Address the audience as "you" where appropriate.
- Keep the tone clear, confident, inclusive, professional, and humble.
- Avoid jargon, hype, buzzwords, exaggeration, and vague claims.
- Be structured, precise, and useful.

## Visual Defaults

When no other specific instruction is given, use this palette:

- Background: Pure White `#FFFFFF`
- Primary text: Burnout Rubber `#2E2E2E`
- Structural/supporting elements: Circuit Slate `#4B5568`
- Accent/highlight: Ignition Mango `#FF8700`

Approximate proportions:

- Pure White `#FFFFFF`: `60%`
- Burnout Rubber `#2E2E2E` / Circuit Slate `#4B5568`: `30%`
- Ignition Mango `#FF8700`: `10%`

## Design Constraints

- Always check accessibility contrast.
- Keep content left-aligned unless a specific layout requires otherwise.
- Avoid all-caps headlines.
- Use generous spacing.
- Use Barlow or Barlow Condensed for headlines where applicable.
- Use Inter for body text where applicable.
- Use technical line art with photorealistic elements for imagery.
- Use Ignition Mango `#FF8700` sparingly as an accent.

## Approval Constraints

Marketing approval is required for:

- Monochrome product logos
- Monochrome solution lockups
- Monochrome badges
- New or exceptional product lockup cases
- Any non-standard brand application
