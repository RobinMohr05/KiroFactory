## 5. Core Brand Elements

## 5.1 Logo System

### Primary Logo

The primary logo consists of the TecAlliance logomark alongside the company name. It is the main identifier of the brand and should be used in most instances to ensure full brand recognition.

#### Primary Logo Minimum Sizes

- **Print:** 20 mm wide minimum
- **Digital:** 120 px wide minimum

#### Primary Logo Usage Rules

- Maintain proper clear space.
- Maintain legibility across all applications.
- Use approved logo versions only.
- Do not distort, recolour, rotate, stretch, compress, rearrange, or alter the logo.

### Logo Mark

The logo mark is a standalone representation of the TecAlliance brand identity. It is used when space is limited or when brand recognition has already been established.

#### Logo Mark Construction

- Uses the golden ratio.
- `B = A / 1.618`
- `C = B / 1.618`

### Product Logos

Product logos exist for:

- TecDoc
- TecCom
- TecRMI

Product logos integrate each product name into the logo design, reinforcing the connection between each product and the overall brand.

#### Product Logo Small-Scale Rule

For small-scale applications, use the logo without text when the logo is:

- **Digital:** `≤ 65 px`
- **Print:** `≤ 15 mm`

### Product Logo Monochrome Rule

Full-colour product logos may not provide sufficient visibility on dark backgrounds. A monochrome version may be used only as an exception under these conditions:

- Dark background with a minimum contrast ratio of `7.5:1`.
- Solid background only.
- No contrasted gradients, patterns, or additional graphic elements.
- Monochrome versions are not standard assets.
- Prior alignment with the Marketing department is required.

### Logo Combination Rule

The TecAlliance logo should always appear alongside any product logo.

When format allows:

- Display both logos together.
- Separate them with a vertical line.

For special formats:

- Logos may be placed separately within the layout.
- The TecAlliance logo may appear smaller than the solution logo if the format requires it, but it must remain clearly visible.

### Solution Lockups

Product representations follow a unified lockup system:

- Product name on the first line.
- Product name typography: **Barlow Condensed Semibold**.
- Solution or programme below in smaller size.
- Solution or programme typography: **Inter Light**.
- Products outside a defined product family are positioned under TecAlliance.
- New or exceptional cases require Marketing approval.

- **Pantone:** `Pantone White`

Secondary colours are linked to specific product identities. They are primarily used in product logos and stock images. Outside these contexts, the TecAlliance corporate colour palette should remain the primary reference.

#### Blueprint

- **Role:** Technical, authoritative, instantly recognisable product colour.
- **Hex:** `#0A74DA`
- **RGB:** `R10 G116 B218`

- **Role:** Swiftness, action, and forward momentum.
- **Hex:** `#D22630`
- **RGB:** `R210 G38 B48`
- **CMYK:** `C0 M82 Y77 K18`

- **Role:** Crisp, technical, cohesive product colour.
- **Hex:** `#007A87`
- **RGB:** `R0 G122 B135`
- **CMYK:** `C100 M10 Y30 K25`
- **Pantone:** `Pantone 7474 C`

### Colour Hierarchy

The corporate TecAlliance colours define the overall visual language of the brand.

- **Pure White** `#FFFFFF` should act as the dominant base colour: approximately `60%` of the design.
- **Burnout Rubber** `#2E2E2E` and/or dark structural tones such as **Circuit Slate** `#4B5568` provide structure and readability: approximately `30%` of the design.
- **Ignition Mango** `#FF8700` is used selectively as an accent colour to highlight key elements: approximately `10%` of the design.

### Colour Usage Rules for AI Agents

When generating brand-compliant content:

1. Use **Pure White** `#FFFFFF` as the main background colour whenever possible.
2. Use **Burnout Rubber** `#2E2E2E` for primary typography.
3. Use **Circuit Slate** `#4B5568` for structured areas, supporting panels, dark interface elements, and secondary hierarchy.
4. Use **Ignition Mango** `#FF8700` sparingly for highlights, calls to action, accents, and emphasis.
5. Use product colours only when representing the relevant product identity:
   - TecDoc / Blueprint: `#0A74DA`
   - TecCom / Rapid Red: `#D22630`
   - TecRMI / Torque Teal: `#007A87`
6. Do not substitute approximate colours.
7. Do not invent additional brand colours unless explicitly requested and approved.
8. Ensure all foreground/background combinations meet accessibility contrast requirements.

Used for the TecAlliance primary logo. It provides a bold, modern, distinctive identity and may also be used for selected headlines or slogans.

Used for headlines and product names. Barlow provides a structured, impactful appearance and supports clarity in key messaging.

Used for general content and extended text. Inter provides readability and accessibility across print and digital platforms.

- **Weights:** Light, Regular, Bold
- **Use cases:** Body copy, long-form text, interface text

## 5.4 Layout System

The layout system ensures visual consistency across communication materials. It defines how content is structured for clarity, readability, accessibility, and brand alignment.

### Layout Principles

- Clean
- Professional
- Accessible
- Balanced
- Structured
- Easy to read

### Layout Construction

The TecAlliance logo is used as the foundational guide for layout calculations.

#### Step 1: Determine Y

- **Portrait formats:** `Y = document width / 5`
- **Landscape formats:** `Y = document width / 7`

#### Step 2: Determine X

After Y is known, X is derived from the logo construction and is used to set consistent margins. The brand book defines X visually based on the logo system.

#### Portrait Margins

- Use margins equal to `1.5X` on all sides.

#### Landscape Margins

- Left margin: `1.5X`
- Right margin: `1.5X`
- Top margin: `1X`
- Bottom margin: `1X`

#### Header and Footer Spacing

When header or footer content exists:

- Apply the defined top and bottom margins.
- Leave the same amount of space between header/footer and the main content area.

### Text Layout

- Use left alignment for natural reading flow.
- Use generous line spacing and paragraph spacing.
- Maintain clear hierarchy between headlines and body text.
- Avoid all-caps headlines.
- Keep layouts approachable and clear.

### Overall Imagery Style

TecAlliance uses a visual style based on AI technologies combined with careful refinement. The imagery should create a unified and recognisable brand identity across channels.

The preferred imagery style combines:

- Primary corporate colours:
  - Ignition Mango `#FF8700`

- Dynamic Ignition Mango accents `#FF8700`

- Backgrounds should remain in technical line art style.
- Colour tones should align with the corporate palette:
  - Ignition Mango `#FF8700`

Real imagery may be used for:

- Events
- Meetings
- Quotes
- Authentic documentation

Rules:

- Use the highest possible quality.
- Select clean, professional images.
- Ensure visual alignment with the brand.
- Where feasible, remove backgrounds and replace them with line-art backgrounds and colour accents.
- Alternatively, frame imagery within clean rectangular or square shapes.

### Product Visual Identity

TecAlliance is the leading corporate brand, while solutions retain their own recognisable identities.

Product colours are used exclusively within product logos and stock imagery:

- TecDoc / Blueprint: `#0A74DA`
- TecCom / Rapid Red: `#D22630`

### Mockups and Screenshots

- Use device mockups only when they provide clear contextual value.
- Prefer clean sections of screenshots without unnecessary device frames.
- Use rounded corners where appropriate.
- Highlight the most relevant part of the interface.
- Avoid crowded or unreadable screens.

## 6.1 Voice

TecAlliance communication is clear, professional, and driven by expertise in data solutions. It uses approachable, informative language that reflects the brand values and maintains a consistent, customer-focused approach.

### Communication Standards

- **Tone:** Clear, confident, inclusive
- **Style:** Informative, structured, purpose-driven
- **Language:** British English
- **Voice:** Use “we” for TecAlliance
- **Audience address:** Use “you” where context allows
- **Principle:** No jargon, no exaggeration, clarity and precision above all

### Writing Rules for AI Agents

When writing as TecAlliance:

1. Use British English spelling.
2. Write from the perspective of “we”.
3. Address the reader as “you” when appropriate.
4. Be clear, structured, and purposeful.
5. Communicate with conviction, not arrogance.
6. Avoid buzzwords, inflated language, and vague claims.
7. Make every message useful and action-oriented.
8. Avoid empty phrases.
9. Be customer-aware without being overly promotional.

## 7. Brand Applications

## 7.1 Co-Branding

TecAlliance uses standardised branding in collaborations to ensure clear and consistent communication.

All brand principles apply to co-branded materials, even where not explicitly restated.

### Co-Branding Types

#### Endorsement

TecAlliance acknowledges or supports a collaborator, usually in an unequally weighted collaboration.

Examples:

- Powered by
- In cooperation with
- Supported by

#### Co-Communication

An equal collaboration where both brands are represented with balanced visibility. Each brand keeps its own voice and identity.

#### Co-Creation

A collaboration that results in a jointly developed product or service. Guidelines are established collaboratively with the other company.

### Co-Branding Decision Logic

Use co-branding only when it has a clear purpose and protects the reputation and appearance of the TecAlliance brand.

Questions to consider:

1. Does TecAlliance benefit from communicating the collaboration?
2. Do both parties contribute at an equal level?
3. Is TecAlliance playing the main role?
4. Is there a common product, service, event, or similar result?
5. Is TecAlliance the publisher of the communication medium?

## 8. Accessibility

Accessibility ensures that all users can interact with digital content effectively.

TecAlliance aligns materials with:

- European Accessibility Act requirements
- EN 301 549
- WCAG 2.2 Level AA

### Contrast Requirements

- Small text: minimum contrast ratio `4.5:1`
- Large text: minimum contrast ratio `3:1`
- UI components: minimum contrast ratio `3:1`

### Important Brand Contrast Values Mentioned

The brand book includes the following contrast examples:

- `5.63:1`
- `1.8:1`
- `2.92:1`
- `2.62:1`
- `2.67:1`
- `2.4:1`
- `7.52:1`
- `4.64:1`
- `5.17:1`
- `5.08:1`

AI agents must not assume all brand colour combinations are accessible. Validate contrast before finalising any design.

### Accessibility Rules for AI Agents

1. Check colour contrast for all text.
2. Use at least `4.5:1` for small text.
3. Use at least `3:1` for large text and UI components.
4. Prefer high-contrast combinations involving:
   - Burnout Rubber `#2E2E2E`
   - Pure White `#FFFFFF`
   - Circuit Slate `#4B5568`
5. Use Ignition Mango `#FF8700` mainly as an accent, not as long-form text.
6. Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.

# 12. AI Agent Instruction Set

Use the following rules when generating content for or about TecAlliance.

## 12.1 Brand Meaning

- Present TecAlliance as a neutral infrastructure provider for the IAM.
- Emphasise connectivity, interoperability, standards, neutral data, and trusted enablement.
- Do not position TecAlliance as competing with market participants.
- Avoid presenting TecAlliance as a single end-solution provider. It enables others to build and operate effectively.

## 12.2 Voice and Writing

- Use British English.
- Write as “we” when speaking on behalf of TecAlliance.
- Address the audience as “you” where appropriate.
- Keep the tone clear, confident, inclusive, professional, and humble.
- Avoid jargon, hype, buzzwords, exaggeration, and vague claims.
- Be structured, precise, and useful.

## 12.3 Visual Defaults

When no other specific instruction is given, use this palette:

- Background: Pure White `#FFFFFF`
- Primary text: Burnout Rubber `#2E2E2E`
- Structural/supporting elements: Circuit Slate `#4B5568`
- Accent/highlight: Ignition Mango `#FF8700`

Use approximate proportions:

- Pure White `#FFFFFF`: `60%`
- Burnout Rubber `#2E2E2E` / Circuit Slate `#4B5568`: `30%`
- Ignition Mango `#FF8700`: `10%`

## 12.4 Product Colour Mapping

Use exact product colour values:

- TecDoc: Blueprint `#0A74DA`, `R10 G116 B218`, `C95 M47 Y0 K15`, `Pantone 2935 C`
- TecCom: Rapid Red `#D22630`, `R210 G38 B48`, `C0 M82 Y77 K18`, `Pantone 1797 C`
- TecRMI: Torque Teal `#007A87`, `R0 G122 B135`, `C100 M10 Y30 K25`, `Pantone 7474 C`

## 12.5 Corporate Colour Mapping

Use exact corporate colour values:

- Ignition Mango `#FF8700`, `R255 G135 B0`, `C0 M47 Y100 K0`, `Pantone 2012 C`
- Circuit Slate `#4B5568`, `R74 G85 B104`, `C29 M18 Y0 K59`, `Pantone 432 C`
- Burnout Rubber `#2E2E2E`, `R46 G46 B46`, `C0 M0 Y0 K95`, `Pantone Black 7 C`

## 12.6 Design Constraints

- Always check accessibility contrast.
- Keep content left-aligned unless a specific layout requires otherwise.
- Avoid all-caps headlines.
- Use generous spacing.
- Use Barlow or Barlow Condensed for headlines where applicable.
- Use Inter for body text where applicable.
- Use technical line art with photorealistic elements for imagery.
- Use Ignition Mango `#FF8700` sparingly as an accent.

## 12.7 Approval Constraints

Marketing approval is required for:

- Monochrome product logos
- Monochrome solution lockups
- Monochrome badges
- New or exceptional product lockup cases
- Any non-standard brand application
